Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

My Creative Realities ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website mycreativerealities.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and associated services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you submit your email address through our website forms:

  • Email address: Collected when you sign up to receive free content or updates.

We do not collect payment information, physical addresses, or any sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address solely to:

  • Send you the free content you requested (such as our Express Edition guide)
  • Send occasional updates about new books, content, and promotions from My Creative Realities
  • Improve our communications based on engagement

We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Pinterest API Usage

My Creative Realities uses the Pinterest API to publish content to our own Pinterest account. Regarding our use of Pinterest data and the Pinterest API:

  • We use the Pinterest API solely to post content to our own authenticated Pinterest account on behalf of My Creative Realities.
  • We do not collect, store, or process any personal data belonging to Pinterest users other than the authenticated account owner.
  • We do not resell, redistribute, or share Pinterest content or Pinterest-derived data with any third parties.
  • When a user disconnects Pinterest from our application, all Pinterest-derived data associated with that session is deleted and not retained.
  • My Creative Realities and this application are not endorsed by, affiliated with, or sponsored by Pinterest, Inc.

4. TikTok API Usage

My Creative Realities uses TikTok's official Content Posting API to publish video content to our own TikTok account. This integration does not collect, store, access, or process any personal data belonging to TikTok users other than the authenticated account owner. No TikTok user data is shared with any third party.

5. Email Communications

All email communications are sent via Gmail automation. You may unsubscribe from our email list at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we send you.

6. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to improve your browsing experience. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

7. Third-Party Services

We link to third-party websites including Amazon, Pinterest, and TikTok. These sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you remain subscribed to our communications. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at keptbetter@gmail.com.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at keptbetter@gmail.com.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at keptbetter@gmail.com.
